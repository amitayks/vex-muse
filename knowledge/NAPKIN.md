# Knowledge Base

## What is this?
Muse's studio memory: the reference compendium that every music-video project
draws from and grows, plus one note per finished project or lab. Method does not
live here — it lives in `.pi/skills/` (start at `mv-director`).

## Key topics
- **references/** — the compendium (grown by skill `reference-mesh`): music-video
  grammar, K-pop attention direction, kinetic lyric typography, anti-slop tells,
  internet-brutalism inserts, and case studies ([[claude-pop-case]],
  [[escape-velocity-case]], [[code-motion-showreels]]: the quality bar is code-made
  motion, not video-model output). Model and tool field notes
  ([[generation-field-notes]], [[tool-selection]]: HyperFrames / Remotion / SVG
  morphing; [[music-tools]]: fetching, editing and generating songs;
  [[voice-casting]]: narrated speech and accented character voices). Zeitgeist
  dossiers per audience are added here with a `seen` date and a half-life.
  Brand dossiers too: [[shift-labs-brand]] backs skill `shift-labs-design`;
  interactive model: [[vex-interactive-explainers]].
- **projects/** — one note per delivered project or lab: brief in one paragraph,
  what worked, what didn't, spend, links to the final render.
- **principal.md** — who Muse works for and their standing policies.
