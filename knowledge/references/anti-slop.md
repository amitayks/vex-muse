# Anti-slop — what makes generated work look generated

The design-literate timeline dunks on these on sight. Treat each as a defect.

**Image/plate tells**
- Pixar/"3D mascot" plastic skin, big glossy eyes, subsurface-glow everything.
- Center-framed subject, symmetric, shallow DOF bokeh, teal-orange grade.
- Over-detailed noise: every surface textured, no rest area for the eye.
- Rim light + volumetric god rays + lens flare on unrelated shots.
- Same-face syndrome across cast; hands with melted fingers; text gibberish.
- "Epic" scale with no event; slow-motion hair wind as filler.
- Style drift shot to shot (lighting model, line weight, palette).

**Motion tells**
- Everything moving at one floaty speed; no holds; no anticipation.
- Camera orbit for no reason; morphing transitions as a default.
- Lip flap without syllable shapes; eyes that never lead the head.
- **Slide-deck motion:** cuts on a fixed clock (e.g. every 2.5 s) that
  ignores the music, one layout reused every slide (headline left, 3D blob
  right), generic slogans, and objects that rotate but never transform.
  It reads as a website hero animated into a deck
  ([[code-motion-showreels]], the GPT half of the comparison).
- **Trend echo:** reusing the look of whatever just went viral (today: red dot on
  near-black, grotesk + italic serif + mono HUD, gizmo in-jokes, dot-as-full-stop; and since
  mid-Sep 2026 dot-particle solids, prompt-box typing, tweet cards, CRT/terminal green, mint or
  lavender on black, gradient-blur end cards, the spinning hypercube).
  Copied taste is slop one month later. Take principles, invent the look. The list goes stale
  weekly: check it live against the launch wall (`whatships.py wall`, [[whatships]]).
- **Default-palette drift:** even a test or lab piece inherits the house palette, and the house
  palette is usually last month's trend. Choose it per piece, outside the current fingerprint.
- Crossfades as the default transition. Strong work transitions with an
  object: iris, match cut, a motif that becomes the next background.

**Counters**
- Pick a medium with a physical logic (paper, ink, risograph, cel, clay,
  collage) and obey it: its textures, its limits, its mistakes.
- Flat staging, graphic silhouettes, deliberate asymmetry, negative space
  for text.
- A restricted palette (4–6 colors + paper) held across the piece.
- Imperfection with rules: boil, misregistration, grain — seeded, not noise.
- Timing with contrast: fast snaps, long holds. Cut on the bar grid and
  accelerate cut density into the end.
- Reference real craft (a named illustrator tradition, a print process) in
  prompts instead of "high quality, 8k, masterpiece".
