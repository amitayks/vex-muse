# Music-video grammar — structure that survives a 3-minute watch

**Macro**
- Sections are acts. Intro = question/mystery; verse = world + protagonist
  want; pre-chorus = pressure; chorus = release on a recurring stage that
  escalates each time; bridge = the twist/breakdown (change medium, palette
  or scale); final chorus = everything at once; outro = the rhyme with the
  opening.
- Escalation per chorus is the spine of retention: same place, bigger
  stakes (scale, crowd, color, speed, destruction).
- A performance thread (protagonist singing to camera) interleaved with a
  narrative thread and an insert thread; switch threads on phrase
  boundaries.

**Micro (from the PDoom/ClaudeAnimationBase lessons)**
- Every shot has an event: first frame ≠ last frame in meaning.
- Reads: list what the viewer must understand per shot, give each time to
  be found, understood and registered; never two important reads at once.
- Fast actions, slow meanings; cause then reaction; the reaction is often
  the payload.
- A transition at every seam, chosen for the story: match cut, cut on
  action, whip with smear, shaped iris, brush wipe, camera carry.
- One world, one palette arc, a motif that pays off, ending rhymes with the
  opening.
- Everything alive: idle motion, camera drift, boil — a frozen frame reads
  as a bug.

**Lip-sync shots**
- Mouth shapes must hit syllable onsets within ±2 frames; open vowels on
  held notes. Medium/close framing only when sync is verified; otherwise
  cut to profile, back, silhouette, or a hand/mic insert.

**Cadence and objects (from [[code-motion-showreels]])**
- Author the timeline in bars and beats, not seconds; cuts and hits on the grid, drift between.
- Cut density is the energy curve: bar-long chapters → beat cuts → half-beat supercut → hard stop →
  a held end card. Keep real holds (20–40 % of frames still).
- Transitions are objects: the song's own motif carries the viewer into the next scene (in the
  showreels a dot became an iris and a full stop — that specific device is now their fingerprint). Crossfade only when the meaning is "this continues".
