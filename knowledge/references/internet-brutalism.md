# Internet brutalism — raw-web inserts inside a crafted piece

A crafted piece that suddenly cuts to raw internet material (a screenshot, a
chart, a quote-tweet, a meme frame) reads as self-aware and native to the
timeline. Done lazily it reads as a slideshow.

**Rules**
- Inserts are rhythmic punctuation: 4–12 frames, on a beat or a lyric
  punch, then straight back to the world. Rarely longer than a bar.
- Frame them as objects: a phone screen, a monitor, a torn printout, a
  window chrome, a sticker — so they belong to the world's physics.
- Degrade deliberately: JPEG crunch, scanline, halftone, misregistration,
  screen moiré — one treatment per piece.
- Stack for acceleration: 1 insert per bar → per beat → per half-beat as the
  song builds; the density itself tells "things are speeding up".
- Recreate rather than rip where rights are unclear: re-typeset a post,
  redraw a chart in our style with the real numbers, redraw a meme frame
  (see reference-mesh rights rules).
- Numbers and charts are the tech audience's memes: exponential curves,
  benchmark bars hitting 100%, "solved" stamps, loss curves — highly
  decodable, highly shareable.
