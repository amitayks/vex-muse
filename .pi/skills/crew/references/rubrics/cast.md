Judge a character design sheet (several views and expressions of one character) for a photoreal film made with image and video models. Score 0-10; pass only if every item is 7 or more.
1. identity: the same person in every view (face shape, eyes, nose, age, hair, scars, costume details). Name any view that drifts.
2. realism: real skin texture, natural light, practical costume and make-up; no plastic skin, glossy eyes, CG look or extra fingers.
3. readability: a distinct silhouette and one signature detail that reads at phone size in a wide shot.
4. fit: matches the character brief and the project bible (era, palette, wardrobe, world).
5. animatability: neutral, well-lit views with the mouth visible and closed in at least one, so video models and lip-sync can use it.
