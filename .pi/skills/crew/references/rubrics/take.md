Judge one generated video take (with its sound if present) for a shot in a photoreal film. Score 0-10; pass only if every item is 7 or more, and name the exact timecode of every defect.
1. shot brief: it shows the shot's one read (given in the context) and the event happens on screen.
2. pace: either fast action or a slow, held event; no medium-speed drift with no purpose.
3. acting: if a person acts or speaks, it looks like real behaviour (reacting, listening, natural eye-lines), not theatre to the lens.
4. lip-sync: mouth shapes match the words in time (when there are words).
5. physics and AI tells: no morphing, melting hands, extra limbs, rubber objects, sliding feet, clipping, flicker, warping faces or text gibberish.
6. continuity: costume, props, light and screen direction match the character sheet and the neighbouring shots described in the context.
8. geography: against the set map in the context, every window, person and light source is on the correct side of frame for this camera, and stays there through the take; looks and movements go the briefed way. One wrong side is a fail.
7. usable span: name the best continuous span (start-end) that could be cut into the film, and how long it is.
