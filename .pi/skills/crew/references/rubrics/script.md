Judge a short-film scene script (about 60 s of screen time) meant to be made with AI image and video models, voice actors re-voiced from AI-actor drivers, and a human-grade edit. Score each item 0-10; pass only if every item is 7 or more.
1. conversation: dialogue between two or more people that sounds like real talk (interruptions, half-sentences, reactions, people answering each other, listeners shown), never one person reciting to camera or explaining the plot.
2. pace: only two speeds - fast action OR slow, held events. Flag any beat that moves at a medium, purposeless speed.
3. one read per beat: each beat has one thing the viewer must understand, in cause -> reaction order, with enough screen time.
4. coverage: the script names shot sizes, eye-lines, who is on screen while someone else speaks, and the sound perspective (inside/outside, intercom/radio/off-screen).
5. producibility: every shot is something current AI video does well in 1-4 s (short shots, locked or mounted cameras, fast subjects across frame, rain/smoke/motion blur hiding detail). Flag shots that need long continuous acting, hands doing fine work, readable text, or crowds in close-up.
6. fit: fits the stated runtime (count words at about 2.5 words per second of talk plus the action beats) and the brief.
7. originality: its own world and characters; not a copy of any named film or series.
8. stakes and ending: something is at risk from the first seconds, it escalates, and the last shot lands an image the viewer remembers.
