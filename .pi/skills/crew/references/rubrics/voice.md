Judge the voice casting of one character in a photoreal film whose dialogue is made with library TTS voices (re-voiced through a voice changer when the actor is on screen). The record is a choice note plus the audition audio clips. Listen to every clip before you score. Score 0-10; pass only if every item is 7 or more.
1. fit: the chosen voice matches the character brief (age, gender, accent, texture and the delivery words) and the channel the audience hears it on (intercom, radio, phone, open air).
2. performance: on the audition line the delivery sounds like a real person at work in that moment, not a narrator, an advert or an audiobook read; the pace fits the line's slot in the script.
3. clean: every word is intelligible and correct; no glitches, artefacts, dropped or added words, or wrong stress on names and numbers.
4. contrast: the voice is distinct from the other characters who share scenes with it (pitch, texture, pace, channel), as far as the note documents them.
5. evidence: at least 3 voices auditioned on the same line with at least 2 takes of the pick; the choice is backed by measurements (transcripts, timing against the slot) and by listening; the voice id, model and settings are recorded so any later line can be re-made.
6. honesty: the note states its limits and claims no check that was not done.
