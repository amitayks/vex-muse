# LEARNINGS — storyboard
One line per lesson, dated, as intent.
- 2026-09-26 The strongest launch films this month (Mirage's horse in ~15 treatments, Cube Motion's one dot system) cohere the tesseract way — one invariant seen through many projections; name the invariant before the sections.
- 2026-10-07 (the principal, on WHAT THE TIDE OWES v1) Pace has two speeds only: fast action OR slow events. No medium-speed middle where things move briskly with no purpose. A beat that is not action gets time to breathe.
- 2026-10-07 (the principal) Dialogue is a conversation between two or more people, never one person reciting to camera. Plan shot/reverse, reactions, overlaps, off-screen answers and listeners on screen while someone else talks, so it plays as natural talk and not as theatre.
- 2026-10-07 (the principal, BACK IN TWELVE look test) A reverse shot put the left-seat pilot's window on frame-left. Spatial logic across cuts matters more than any detail; every set has a per-camera side map, and frames are checked against it.
