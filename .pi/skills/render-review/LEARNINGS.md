Oversize for WhatsApp → send the file on his web chat instead.

- 2026-09-26 "Original" needs a live comparison, not memory: the quick check is our contact sheet beside this fortnight's whatships launch wall (whatships.py wall) — the trend moves weekly.
- 2026-09-26 Cutting between two shots of the same performer reads as a jump when the eyes change height; check eye-line continuity at every performer→performer seam (from Mirage's Tesseract editing guide; the principal approved this one point only).
- 2026-09-27 On the CPU box, HyperFrames `--quality draft` only swaps the encoder (ultrafast CRF 28); screenshot capture (~2.5-3 fps at 1080p, 2 workers) is the whole cost. For long local renders go straight to `--quality looks` (CRF 16): the review copy can be the master if it passes. Multi-worker mp4 needs `HF_CAPTURE_PARALLEL_STREAM=true` or it dumps every frame to disk (~1 MB/frame).
- 2026-09-27 A GSAP/HyperFrames comp that is a pure function of time renders on Modal CPU containers with `scripts/modal_hf.py` (seek the registered timeline + screenshot + per-chunk x264, local lossless concat + master-audio mux): 0.35 s/frame per container, a 5.5-min film in ~5 min on 25 containers vs 96 min locally. Verified parity against the local HyperFrames master: time-aligned, PSNR 35-37 dB, difference = uniform encoder noise (mean 2.5/255), no structural change. Keep `-tune animation` off on vellum/grain textures (it doubled the bitrate).
- 2026-09-27 The whole-film strip (1 frame / 2 s) caught what 110 hand-picked stills missed: three 1.5-2 s blank pages under live narration at scene seams. Always sample the seams at 0.5 s after a strip flags one; an empty page while the narrator talks reads as a dropped shot, not a breath.
